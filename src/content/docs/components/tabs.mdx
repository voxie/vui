Tabs is a row of choices in a track, with a white pill behind the one that is picked. Choosing another tab slides the pill over to it. It is two components. `Tabs` is the track, holds the `v-model`, and handles the keyboard. `Tab` is one choice inside it, and has a `value` and a label.

```vue
<Tabs v-model="view" aria-label="Conversations">
  <Tab value="all">All</Tab>
  <Tab value="open">Open</Tab>
  <Tab value="closed">Closed</Tab>
</Tabs>
```

The `v-model` is the `value` of the picked tab. Every tab is the same width, taken from the widest label, and the track fills whatever it is in.

The track is a `<div role="tablist">` and each tab is a `<button role="tab">`. Give the track a name with `aria-label`, or point `aria-labelledby` at a heading next to it. Tabs draws no panels. Show and hide the content for each tab yourself, off the `v-model`.

### Surface

Tabs works out what it is sitting on for itself, by asking the [Card](/docs/components/card) or [Navbar](/docs/components/navbar) it's inside. The track is the off track of a [Switch](/docs/components/switch#surface), surface for surface. It is slate-300, and steps away from the surface where slate-300 would vanish into it. On white it lightens to slate-200. On `sunken` it goes to slate-400 at 50%. On `dark` it goes the other way, to slate-800, so the tabs read as a well in the panel. The pill is white everywhere.

| Sitting on   | Which is     | Track         |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------- |
| `default`    | white        | slate-200     |
| `glass`      | slate-50     | slate-300     |
| `sunken`     | slate-300/30 | slate-400/50  |
| `dark`       | slate-700    | slate-800     |
| `background` | slate-200    | slate-300     |

<TabsSurfacesExample client:load />

The unpicked labels are slate-600, and slate-300 on `dark` where they sit on the slate-800 track. The picked label is slate-800 on its white pill everywhere.

The track has a shallow inset shadow and a 1px bottom border in white, so it reads as pressed into the surface. The border is solid white on `default` and `glass`, white at 40% on `background`, white at 30% on `sunken`, and white at 10% on `dark`.

The page background is the surface nothing can announce. That's what `sitsOn` is for. An element that paints a background of its own can wrap its contents in [SurfaceProvider](/docs/components/card#a-surface-that-isnt-a-card) instead.

### Size

The `size` prop sets the height of the track, and the text and padding of each tab. The sizes are `sm`, `md` (the default), and `lg`. Each is the height of the labeled [Switch](/docs/components/switch#size) and of a [Button](/docs/components/button#size) at the same size, so the three sit level in a toolbar.

### Disabled

`disabled` on the track flattens it to slate-200 and the pill to slate-50, drops every label to slate-400, and ignores presses. The pill stays where it was, so a disabled set of tabs still shows which one is picked. On `dark` the track is slate-800 at 50%, the pill slate-500, and the labels slate-500 with the picked one slate-300.

`disabled` on a single `Tab` greys that one out and skips it. Clicks do nothing and the arrow keys pass over it.

## Interaction

Hovering an unpicked tab fills it with slate-400 at 40%, slate-500 at 30% on `sunken`, and slate-700 on `dark`. The picked tab has no hover. Pressing any tab shrinks it to 95%, and pressing the picked one shrinks the pill with it. The shrink is `motion-safe`, so it doesn't run for someone who has asked for reduced motion, and a disabled tab doesn't shrink at all.

The pill takes 200ms to slide from one tab to the next on an ease-out curve, quick and without a bounce, in line with [Look and Feel](/docs/foundations/look-and-feel#friendly). The label colors cross over in the same 200ms.

<kbd>Tab</kbd> lands on the picked tab and nowhere else in the track. From there <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move to the next tab and pick it as they go, wrapping at either end, and <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> jump to the first and last. Disabled tabs are skipped. Until something is picked, every tab is in the tab order. Focus draws a blue outline around the tab, two pixels wide at a two pixel offset, and only for keyboard focus. Clicking a tab doesn't ring it.

Until the page has hydrated, the picked tab paints its own white in place of the pill, so server-rendered HTML shows a selection and nothing flashes when the pill takes over.

## Contrast

| Element                    | On            | WCAG 2.x | APCA   |
| -------------------------- | ------------- | -------- | ------ |
| slate-800 label            | white pill    | 14.7:1   | Lc 101 |
| slate-600 label            | slate-200     | 6.1:1    | Lc 72  |
| slate-600 label            | slate-300     | 5.1:1    | Lc 61  |
| slate-600 label            | slate-400/50  | 4.2:1    | Lc 50  |
| slate-600 label            | hovered tab   | 4.6:1    | Lc 56  |
| slate-300 label            | slate-800     | 9.9:1    | Lc 77  |
| white pill                 | slate-200     | 1.2:1    | Lc 13  |
| white pill                 | slate-300     | 1.5:1    | Lc 26  |
| white pill                 | slate-400/50  | 1.8:1    | Lc 38  |
| white pill                 | slate-800     | 14.7:1   | Lc 104 |
| slate-200 track            | white         | 1.2:1    | Lc 11  |
| slate-300 track            | slate-50      | 1.4:1    | Lc 20  |
| slate-400/50 track         | sunken        | 1.4:1    | Lc 16  |
| slate-800 track            | dark          | 1.4:1    | Lc 0   |
| slate-400 label            | slate-200     | 2.1:1    | Lc 37  |
| slate-400 label            | slate-50 pill | 2.5:1    | Lc 48  |

Every label clears AA on every surface, and the picked one clears AAA on its pill. The slate-600 label on the `sunken` track is the closest call at 4.2:1.

The pill is what says which tab is picked, and on the light surfaces it doesn't clear on color. White on the slate-200 track is 1.2:1, short of the 3:1 WCAG asks of a control's boundary. The pill's shadow lifts it off the track, and the picked label is two steps darker than the rest, so the state survives without the color. On `dark` the white pill clears at 14.7:1.

The disabled labels run at 2.1:1 and 2.5:1. Disabled controls are exempt from the contrast rules, and they are deliberately quiet.
