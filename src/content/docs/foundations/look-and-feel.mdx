---

Voxie is a dense product that carries a lot of data and complex functionality. The design's job is to make it feel light and approachable. Soft shapes with carefully chosen accents and content that lifts off a quiet page. It should feel like the kind of message the platform sends, friendly and worth reading. 


## Friendly

Surfaces and buttons are heavily rounded. Icons are too but with more subtly so they remain recognizable at small sizes. Ample white space gives the eye places to rest while generating a sense of calm. Accent colors use light fills with dark text. Motion is quick and gentle, nothing bounces. 


## Clear

Grids bring structure. Heavy weight headlines, cards that group content, and clearly labeled inputs bring clarity. Calculated use of color highlights calls to action or important data.
