The Voxie product can be dense and complicated. To counter this, the design strives for a few things: 

## friendly, approachable look
- heavy corner radius
- ample white space
- bright colors
- friendly iconography
- playful animation 

## calculated use of color 
Color is used sparingly to highlight clear call to action buttons or important data. We strive to avoid rainbow screens where everything has a color. Color is one of the most attention grabbing elements in design and we treat it that way.

## strong hierarchy
- heavy weight header fonts
- cards to group related content together
- clear labels on inputs
