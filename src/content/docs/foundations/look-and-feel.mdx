description: Overall visual direction and tone.
order: 3
---

Our product can be dense and complicated. We try to balance that out with
friendly, approachable design. Some of the key elements that help us
accomplish this are minimal gray primary palette with bright accent colors, rounded corners, iconography, and
playful animation. We aim for subtlety in our designs though, so the software
maintains a sense of reliability and usefulness.
