The Voxie product should feel friendly and approachable with a strong sense of clarity. We highlight what's important and guide users in structured way. We do this to provide a counterbalance for areas of the app that can be dense and complicated. This also sets the stage for the types of positive conversations the platform should be hosting. 

## Core Design Elements
- ample white space
- bright colors
- use of grids for structured organization
- heavy corner radius
- friendly iconography
- playful animation 

## Calculated Use of Color 
Color is used sparingly to highlight clear call to action buttons or important data. We strive to avoid rainbow screens where everything has a color. Color is one of the most attention grabbing elements in design and we treat it that way.

## Strong Hierarchy
- heavy weight header fonts
- cards to group related content together
- clear labels on inputs
