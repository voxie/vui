Voxie is a dense product that carries a lot of data and complex functionality. The design's job is to make it feel light and approachable. This is achieved through soft shapes, carefully chosen accents, and content that lifts off a quiet page. It should feel like the kind of message the platform sends, friendly and worth reading.

## Friendly

Surfaces and buttons are heavily rounded. Solid icons also have rounding but more subtly so they stay recognizable at small sizes. Ample white space gives the eye places to rest. Accents use light fill colors with dark text. Motion is quick and gentle, nothing bounces.

## Clear

White [cards](/docs/components/card) group related content over a grey background so information rises up in an organized way. Grids provide the underlying [page structure](/docs/patterns/page-structure). [Headings](/docs/foundations/typography) rely more on weight than size so they stand out even at smaller scales. 

Every input carries a [label](/docs/patterns/forms#labels) so there's never any confusion about what goes in a field.

[Color](/docs/foundations/color) is used sparingly to highlight clear calls to action or important data. Color is one of the most attention grabbing elements in a design. If everything has color, then nothing stands out.
