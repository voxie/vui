Modal

A modal is a panel over the page, for a task that has to be finished or abandoned before anything else happens. It renders at the end of <body> rather than where you wrote it, so no ancestor’s overflow or stacking context can clip it.

Four slots make the panel, top to bottom. header is the title, rendered as an h2. subheader sits under it in the same white bar. The default slot is the body, tinted to slate-50, and the body is the part that scrolls. footer is the action bar, white again and pinned under the body.

<Modal v-model="open" size="md">
  <template #header>Create an API token</template>
  <template #subheader>A token reads and writes on behalf of your team.</template>

  <Input v-model="name" block placeholder="Token name" />

  <template #footer>
    <Button @click="open = false">Cancel</Button>
    <Button color="sky" block @click="create">Create token</Button>
  </template>
</Modal>

v-model is the boolean that opens and closes it. The modal also emits close when it closes itself, for the cases where the open state isn’t the only thing that has to be reset.

Examples

Head and footer

Every region pads its content by 24px and is divided from the next by a slate-100 line. The head and the footer are white, the body is slate-50, so the chrome reads as chrome and the content reads as content.

The body is a glass surface and it says so to everything inside it. An Input or a Select in there picks up the right boundary without being told. The head and the footer are default, so a Button in the action bar comes out slate-100.

The action bar is always there. A modal ends in its actions, so the bar is part of the shape rather than a slot that comes and goes.

The head is the part that can go. Drop header and subheader for a modal whose body speaks for itself, and the body runs to the top edge with no empty bar above it.

The close button floats over the panel rather than sitting in the head, so it survives the head going away. Whichever region is at the top holds its end corner clear of it. With a head that’s 64px of end padding on the title. With no head it’s 56px of top padding on the body, since indenting the body’s whole end edge would pull every row in with it.

A modal with no header needs an aria-label, since there’s no title for the dialog to be named by.

The action bar

The footer is a row. Below sm it stacks, reversed, so the last button written is the one on top and closest to the thumb.

The primary action fills the bar. Give it block and write it last. A secondary button beside it keeps its own width and the primary takes what’s left.

<template #footer>
  <Button @click="open = false">Cancel</Button>
  <Button color="sky" block @click="create">Create token</Button>
</template>

Size

The size prop sets the panel’s maximum width on screens above sm. The sizes are sm, md (the default), lg, xl, and full.

SizeMax width
sm28rem
md36rem
lg48rem
xl64rem
fullthe full window

Below sm the size is ignored. The panel is a sheet on the bottom edge at every size, full width, square across the bottom and rounded across the top. It stops 4rem short of the top of the window, so the page behind it stays visible.

Stacking

A modal can open another one. Mark the one underneath stacked while the second is open, and it scales back, fades, and blurs behind it. A stacked modal ignores Escape and clicks outside, so the only way out is through the modal on top.

Pass :backdrop="false" on the second one. The first modal’s backdrop is already there, and a second would double the dimming.

Closing

dismissible is on by default. Turn it off and the close button goes, along with the padding that was holding room for it. Escape does nothing and a click outside does nothing. The only way out is a control you put in the modal.

Reach for it when leaving halfway through would put something in a broken state. A modal that is merely important still closes.

Backdrop

backdrop is on by default and paints slate-300 at 80% over the page. Turn it off for a modal that sits on top of another one, or where the page behind has to stay readable.

The page still locks either way. Clicking outside still closes it.

Interaction

Opening fades the backdrop in over 200ms and brings the panel up over 300ms, from 4rem below on desktop and from off the bottom edge on mobile. Closing runs at 200ms. Under prefers-reduced-motion the panel only fades.

The page behind stops scrolling while a modal is open, and the width the scrollbar leaves behind is added back as padding so nothing shifts. Nested modals count, so the lock lifts when the last one closes.

Opening focuses the first input, select, or textarea in the body. With no field to focus, the panel takes focus itself. Closing puts focus back on whatever had it before.

Tab and Shift + Tab cycle inside the panel and can’t reach the page behind it. Esc closes it, and only the topmost modal answers, so one press closes one modal. Clicking the backdrop closes it, but only when the press started there, so dragging a selection out of the panel doesn’t.

Methods

A ref on the modal reaches four methods, for the case where the body is a long form and the modal has to move to a part of it.

MethodWhat it does
scrollTo(options)Scrolls the body. Defaults to the top, smoothly.
scrollToElement(element)Scrolls the element into the body, 80px down from the top edge.
focusField(index, scroll)Focuses the nth input, select, or textarea in the body.
focusInvalidField(index, scroll)Focuses the nth element in the body marked aria-invalid="true".

focusField and focusInvalidField scroll to what they focus unless you pass false. Both return false when there’s nothing at that index.

Contrast

ElementOnWCAG 2.x
slate-700 titlewhite head10.4:1
slate-500 body textslate-50 body4.6:1
slate-50 bodywhite head1.05:1
slate-100 dividerwhite1.1:1
white panelbackdrop1.4:1

The title and the body text clear AA. The divider and the panel edge do not, and neither is asked to carry meaning. The step from the white head to the slate-50 body is 1.05:1, which is why the slate-100 line is there as well. The panel is told apart from the page by the backdrop’s dimming and the shadow under it, not by the 1.4:1 step in lightness.
